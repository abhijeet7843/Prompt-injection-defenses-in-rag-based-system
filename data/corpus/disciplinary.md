# Disciplinary committee

The disciplinary committee handles cases of misconduct, including cheating in exams, damage to property and violation of hostel rules.

The disciplinary committee is chaired by Prof. Arvind Rao. It also includes two faculty members, the chief warden and one student representative.

A student called before the committee is informed in writing at least three days before the hearing and may bring a faculty member as an adviser.

The committee can issue a warning, impose a fine, suspend a student for up to one semester, or recommend expulsion to the director. Decisions can be appealed to the director within fifteen days.

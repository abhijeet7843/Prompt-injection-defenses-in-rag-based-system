# Swimming pool

The 25-metre swimming pool is next to the sports complex. The swimming pool is open from March to October and stays closed during the winter months.

Students must show a medical fitness certificate from the health centre before their first swim. A swimming cap and proper swimwear are mandatory.

A lifeguard is on duty during all open hours, which are 6 am to 8 am and 4 pm to 7 pm. Beginners can join free coaching sessions on Tuesday and Thursday evenings.

Food, glass bottles and phones are not allowed on the pool deck.

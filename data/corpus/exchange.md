# Semester exchange programme

Northbridge Institute has exchange agreements with fourteen universities in Europe and Asia. Students spend one semester abroad and pay tuition only at Northbridge.

To apply for the semester exchange programme, a student needs a minimum CGPA of 7.5 and must be in the third year at the time of the exchange.

Applications are made to the international relations office by 15 October for the following spring semester. Shortlisted students are interviewed in November.

Credits earned abroad are transferred after approval by the department. Grades from the partner university are recorded as pass or fail.

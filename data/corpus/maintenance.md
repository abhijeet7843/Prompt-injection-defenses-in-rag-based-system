# Hostel maintenance

Repairs in hostel rooms, such as a broken fan, a leaking tap or a faulty light, are reported on the NB Fix app. Each complaint gets a ticket number.

Electrical and plumbing complaints are usually fixed within 48 hours. Furniture repairs can take up to a week.

Students must be present in the room, or leave the key with the hostel office, when the maintenance staff visit.

Damage caused by misuse is charged to the students living in the room.

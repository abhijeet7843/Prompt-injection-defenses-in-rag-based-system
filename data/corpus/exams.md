# Examinations

End-semester examinations for the autumn semester begin on 1 December. The full timetable is published on the student portal three weeks before the first exam.

Students need at least 75 percent attendance in a course to sit its end-semester exam. Students below this limit can appeal to their head of department with a medical certificate.

Re-evaluation of an answer sheet costs 1,000 rupees per course and must be requested within seven days of results being declared.

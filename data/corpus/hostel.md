# Hostel rules

Hostel gates close at 11 pm. Students returning later must sign the late-entry register at the warden's office and give a reason.

Guests are allowed in the visitors' lounge between 4 pm and 8 pm. Guests may not enter residential floors.

Hostel room allocation for the new academic year is announced on the student portal in the first week of July. Room change requests are handled by the chief warden, Dr. Meera Sethi.

# Cultural festival

Aurora is the annual cultural festival of Northbridge Institute, held over three days in the last week of January.

The festival includes music and dance competitions, a fashion show, drama, literary events and a pro-night with a guest performer. Students from more than sixty colleges take part.

Northbridge students get free entry with their ID card. Outside participants register online and pay a fee of 300 rupees.

Volunteers for Aurora are selected in October through the cultural council.

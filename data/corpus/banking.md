# Banking on campus

Union Ridge Bank has a branch on campus in the shopping complex, open from 10 am to 4 pm on weekdays and 10 am to 1 pm on Saturdays.

New students can open a zero-balance student account at the branch with their admission letter, ID card and a bonafide certificate.

There are three ATMs on campus: at the shopping complex, near Gate 2 and next to the girls' hostel. All three accept cards from any bank.

The scholarship office pays scholarships only into accounts in the student's own name.

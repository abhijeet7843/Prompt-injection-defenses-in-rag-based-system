# Summer internships

Every B.Tech student must complete a summer internship after the third year. The internship carries four credits.

The mandatory summer internship must be at least eight weeks long. It can be done at a company, a research lab or another university.

Students submit an offer letter to their department before the end of the spring semester. After the internship, they submit a report and a certificate, and give a short presentation in the first month of the autumn semester.

Internships found through the placement cell are listed on the placement portal from January onwards.

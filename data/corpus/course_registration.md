# Course registration

Course registration for each semester happens on the student portal during the first week of classes. Students choose their courses after meeting their faculty advisor.

A student must register for at least 16 credits and at most 26 credits in a semester. Registering for more than 26 credits needs written approval from the head of department and is allowed only for students with a CGPA above 8.0.

Students who miss the registration week can register late in the second week by paying a late registration fine of 500 rupees. No registration is accepted after the second week.

Core courses are registered automatically. Students only select their electives, which are filled on a first-come, first-served basis.

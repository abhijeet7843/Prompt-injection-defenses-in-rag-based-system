# Vehicle parking

Students who bring a vehicle to campus must register it with the security office and display a parking sticker.

A two-wheeler parking sticker costs 600 rupees per year. Students are not allowed to park four-wheelers on campus, except with a medical exemption.

Two-wheelers must be parked in the marked area behind the library. Vehicles parked elsewhere are locked by security and released after a fine of 200 rupees.

The speed limit on campus roads is 20 km per hour, and helmets are compulsory.

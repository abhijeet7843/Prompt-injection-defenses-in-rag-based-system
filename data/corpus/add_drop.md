# Adding and dropping courses

Students can add a new course during the first two weeks of the semester, if seats are available and the timetable has no clash.

A course can be dropped without any record on the transcript until the end of week 6. Dropping a course between week 7 and week 10 leaves a W grade on the transcript, which does not affect the CGPA.

No course can be dropped after week 10. Students who stop attending after that point receive an F grade.

All add and drop requests are made on the student portal and must be approved by the course instructor and the faculty advisor.

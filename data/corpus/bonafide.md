# Bonafide certificates

A bonafide certificate confirms that a student is currently enrolled at Northbridge Institute. It is often needed for bank accounts, passports, scholarships and railway concessions.

Bonafide certificates are issued by the Dean of Students office in the Administration Building. Students submit a request form along with a copy of their ID card.

The certificate is free of charge and is ready for collection within two working days. Students must collect it in person; it is not sent by email.

Certificates for visa applications must state the purpose clearly. Mention the embassy name on the request form so it can be printed on the certificate.

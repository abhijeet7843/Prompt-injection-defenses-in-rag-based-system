# Student counselling

The student wellbeing centre offers free and confidential counselling for stress, homesickness, relationships, exam anxiety and other concerns.

The counselling rooms are in Block F, room 12, next to the health centre. Two counsellors are available from 10 am to 6 pm on weekdays.

To book an appointment with a counsellor, students email wellbeing@northbridge.edu or walk in during office hours. Appointments are usually available within three days.

What students share in counselling is not reported to the department or to parents, except where someone's safety is at risk.

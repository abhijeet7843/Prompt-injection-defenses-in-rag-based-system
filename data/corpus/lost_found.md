# Lost and found

Items found anywhere on campus are handed over to the lost and found counter at the security office at Gate 1. The counter is staffed around the clock.

To claim an item, students describe it and show their ID card. Phones and laptops are returned only after the owner unlocks them in front of the staff.

Unclaimed items are kept for 30 days. After that, clothes and books are donated, and electronics are handed to the administration.

Students who lose a valuable item can also post a notice on the lost and found board in the student activity centre.

# Clubs and societies

Northbridge Institute has more than forty student clubs, covering coding, robotics, music, dance, drama, photography, debating and entrepreneurship.

New students can meet every club at the club fair held in the second week of August. Membership applications open right after the fair.

A student can be a member of at most three clubs at a time, so that club work does not affect studies. Office bearers can hold a post in only one club.

Each club gets a yearly budget from the student council and must submit an activity report at the end of the year.

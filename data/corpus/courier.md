# Post and courier

All letters and courier parcels for students are delivered to the campus mail room in Block B, not to the hostels.

Students collect courier parcels from the mail room between 11 am and 5 pm on weekdays by showing their ID card. A text message is sent when a parcel arrives.

Parcels not collected within 14 days are returned to the sender.

A post office counter in the shopping complex sells stamps and handles speed post and registered letters.

# Hostel mess timings

All hostel residents eat at the central mess. Breakfast is served from 7 am to 9 am, lunch from 12 pm to 2 pm, and evening snacks from 5 pm to 6 pm.

Dinner is served from 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm. On Sundays, breakfast starts one hour later, at 8 am.

The weekly menu is decided by the mess committee, which has two student representatives from each hostel. Suggestions can be dropped in the box at the mess entrance.

Students with medical diet needs can ask for a special meal by submitting a doctor's note to the mess manager.

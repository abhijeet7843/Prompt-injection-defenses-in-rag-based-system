# Summer term

A six-week summer term runs from the last week of May to the first week of July. It is meant for repeating failed courses and for catching up on credits.

A student can take at most two courses in the summer term. Each course costs 4,000 rupees, in addition to hostel charges for students who stay on campus.

Summer term courses have the same exams and grading as regular semesters, but classes are held every day.

Registration for the summer term opens on the student portal in the first week of May.

# Convocation

The annual convocation is held in the second week of March in the main auditorium. Graduating students receive their degrees from the chief guest.

Students who want to attend must register on the student portal by 31 January. The convocation registration fee is 3,000 rupees, which covers the gown, the degree folder and lunch.

Gowns are collected from the Dean of Academics office two days before the ceremony and returned immediately after it. A late return is fined 500 rupees.

Students who do not attend can collect their degree certificate from the examination office from April onwards, or ask for it to be posted for a fee of 200 rupees.

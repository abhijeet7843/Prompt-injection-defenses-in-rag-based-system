# Internal Complaints Committee

The Internal Complaints Committee deals with complaints of sexual harassment involving students, faculty or staff. Its members include faculty, staff, a student representative and an external member.

Students can contact the Internal Complaints Committee by email at icc@northbridge.edu, or by meeting any committee member in person.

All complaints are handled confidentially. The committee completes its inquiry within 90 days and can recommend action against the person found responsible.

Retaliation against anyone who files a complaint or acts as a witness is itself treated as misconduct.

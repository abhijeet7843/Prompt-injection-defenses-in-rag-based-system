# Tuition fees and payment

Tuition for the autumn semester must be paid by 15 August. Students who pay after the deadline are charged a late fee of 2,500 rupees.

Fees can be paid online through the student portal using net banking or UPI. Card payments are not accepted. A receipt is emailed within two working days of payment.

Students facing financial difficulty can apply for a fee instalment plan at the accounts office in Block C before the deadline. The plan allows the fee to be split into three monthly payments.

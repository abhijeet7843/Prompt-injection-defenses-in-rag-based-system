# Software licences

The institute provides free software licences for MATLAB and Microsoft Office to all enrolled students. Licences are activated with the campus email account.

Licences stay active until graduation and are switched off one month after the student leaves the institute.

Specialised software such as circuit simulators and CAD tools is installed on the computers in the central computing lab and cannot be used on personal laptops.

Problems with activation are handled by the IT help desk.

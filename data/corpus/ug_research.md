# Undergraduate research programme

The Undergraduate Research Programme lets second- and third-year students work on a research project with a faculty member for one semester or one summer.

Students selected for the programme receive a monthly stipend of 5,000 rupees. Projects that lead to a published paper get an additional award of 10,000 rupees.

Applications open in November for spring projects and in March for summer projects. Students apply with a one-page proposal signed by the faculty supervisor.

At the end of the project, each student presents a poster at the annual research day in April.

# Campus transport

The campus shuttle runs every 20 minutes between the main gate and the Sector 9 metro station from 7 am to 9 pm.

A semester bus pass costs 1,800 rupees and can be bought at the transport office near Gate 2. Students without a pass pay 20 rupees per ride.

Bicycles can be rented from the stand outside the sports complex for 50 rupees a day.

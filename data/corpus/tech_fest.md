# Technical festival

Techtonic is the annual technical festival of Northbridge Institute. The technical festival is held every February over a weekend.

Events include a 24-hour hackathon, robotics contests, a coding championship, workshops and talks by people from industry.

Teams of up to four students can register for each event. The hackathon has a total prize pool of 2 lakh rupees.

Sponsorship and event planning are handled by the Techtonic core team, which recruits second-year students in September.

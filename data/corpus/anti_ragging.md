# Anti-ragging policy

Ragging in any form is a serious offence at Northbridge Institute. It includes teasing, bullying, forcing someone to do something against their will, and any act that causes physical or mental harm.

Every student and parent signs an anti-ragging undertaking at the time of admission.

Students who face or witness ragging can call the anti-ragging helpline on extension 4999, which is answered at all hours, or report to any member of the anti-ragging squad.

Proven cases lead to suspension or expulsion, and serious cases are reported to the police.

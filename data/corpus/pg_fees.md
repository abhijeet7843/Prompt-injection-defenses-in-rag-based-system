# Postgraduate tuition fees

Tuition for M.Tech and MBA students in the autumn semester must be paid by 31 August. A late fee of 3,500 rupees applies after that date.

Postgraduate students pay through the same student portal as undergraduates. Teaching assistants can ask for the fee to be deducted from their stipend.

Instalment plans for postgraduate students allow the fee to be split into two payments, approved by the accounts office in Block C.

# Central library

The central library is open from 8 am to 10 pm on weekdays and from 10 am to 6 pm on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books at a time for 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to six books for 30 days. The fine for a late return is 5 rupees per book per day.

Group study rooms on the second floor can be booked for up to two hours through the library desk.

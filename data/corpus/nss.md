# National Service Scheme

The National Service Scheme (NSS) unit organises community work such as teaching in village schools, blood donation camps, tree planting and cleanliness drives.

First-year students can enrol in NSS in August. To complete NSS, a student must log 120 hours of community service over two years.

Students who complete NSS receive a certificate and one credit, which counts towards the open elective requirement.

The NSS office is in the student activity centre and is coordinated by Dr. Kavita Menon.

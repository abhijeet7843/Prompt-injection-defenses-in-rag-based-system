# E-learning portal

The institute's e-learning portal is called NB Learn. Instructors use it to share lecture slides, recorded lectures, assignments and quiz results.

Students log in to NB Learn with their campus email account. Each registered course appears on the dashboard automatically within a day of registration.

Assignments are submitted on NB Learn before the deadline shown on the course page. Late submissions are marked late and may lose marks as decided by the instructor.

The portal can be used on a phone through the NB Learn app.

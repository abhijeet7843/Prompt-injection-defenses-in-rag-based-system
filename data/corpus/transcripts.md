# Official transcripts

An official transcript lists every course, grade and the CGPA, signed by the Controller of Examinations. It is needed for higher studies and many job applications.

Transcripts are requested through the student portal. Each copy costs 500 rupees, and sealed envelopes for universities abroad cost an extra 100 rupees per envelope.

The examination office issues an official transcript within ten working days of the request. Urgent requests can be completed in three working days for an additional fee of 1,000 rupees.

Students with unpaid dues to the library or the hostel cannot receive a transcript until the dues are cleared.

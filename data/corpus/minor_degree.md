# Minor degrees

Students can earn a minor in a subject outside their main branch, such as economics, data science or design.

A minor degree needs 20 credits of courses from the minor's list, taken in addition to the credits required for the main degree.

Students apply for a minor at the start of the third semester. A CGPA of at least 7.0 is required to apply and must be kept above 6.5 to stay in the programme.

The minor is shown on the degree certificate and the final transcript once all its credits are completed.

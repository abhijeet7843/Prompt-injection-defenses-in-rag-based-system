# Guest house

Parents and visitors of students can stay at the institute guest house near the main gate. Rooms must be booked at least three days in advance through the guest house office.

A guest house room for parents costs 1,200 rupees per night, including breakfast. Rooms for official visitors are booked by departments and charged separately.

Guests can stay for a maximum of five nights at a time. Check-in is from 12 pm and check-out is by 11 am.

Students are not allowed to stay overnight in the guest house.

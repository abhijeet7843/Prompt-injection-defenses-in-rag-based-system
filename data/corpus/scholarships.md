# Scholarships

The Merit Scholarship covers 50 percent of tuition for students with a CGPA of 9.0 or higher in the previous year. It is renewed each year if the CGPA stays above 9.0.

The Need-Based Grant is for students whose family income is below 6 lakh rupees a year. Applications open on 1 September and close on 30 September.

Scholarship applications are submitted through the student portal and reviewed by the scholarship committee within six weeks.

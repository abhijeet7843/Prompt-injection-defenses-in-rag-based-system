# Undergraduate admissions

Applications for the four-year B.Tech programme open on 1 March and close on 30 April. Candidates apply online through the admissions page of the institute website.

The application fee for B.Tech admission is 1,500 rupees and is not refundable. Candidates from reserved categories pay a reduced fee of 750 rupees.

Admission is based on the rank in the Northbridge Entrance Test, held in the second week of May at centres in twelve cities. Results are announced in the first week of June.

Selected candidates must report for document verification with their Class 12 mark sheet, a photo ID and the original admission letter. Seats not confirmed within ten days of the offer are given to waitlisted candidates.

# Student grievance cell

Students can raise complaints about academic matters, facilities or administrative services with the student grievance cell.

Complaints are submitted on the grievance portal or in writing at the cell's office in the Administration Building. Anonymous complaints are accepted.

The grievance cell must respond to every complaint within 15 days. If the student is not satisfied, the complaint is sent to the grievance appeals committee chaired by the Dean of Academics.

Complaints about harassment are handled separately by the Internal Complaints Committee.

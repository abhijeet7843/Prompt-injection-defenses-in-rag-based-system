# Print centre

The student print centre is on the ground floor of Block A, open from 8 am to 8 pm on weekdays and 9 am to 2 pm on Saturdays.

Black-and-white printing costs 1 rupee per page and colour printing costs 8 rupees per page. Spiral binding is available for 30 rupees.

Students can send files from their campus email account to the print centre and collect the printouts later the same day.

Thesis binding takes two working days and must be ordered in advance during the submission season in April and May.

# Mess rebate

Students who are away from campus can claim a rebate on their mess charges. The rebate is credited to the next month's mess bill.

A rebate can be claimed only for an absence of at least five consecutive days. Shorter absences are not eligible.

The rebate application must be submitted to the hostel office at least three days before leaving. Applications made after returning are accepted only for medical emergencies, with a hospital certificate.

The rebate is 120 rupees per day of absence, up to a maximum of 30 days in a semester.

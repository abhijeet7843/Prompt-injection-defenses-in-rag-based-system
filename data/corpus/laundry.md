# Laundry service

Each hostel has a laundry room run by an outside vendor. Clothes are collected on Monday and Thursday mornings and returned two days later.

The laundry plan washes up to 25 pieces of clothing per week for each student, and is included in the hostel fee. Extra pieces cost 10 rupees each.

Blankets and curtains are washed once a semester at no extra charge. Dry cleaning is available at the vendor's counter for an additional fee.

Students should mark their clothes with their room number. The vendor is not responsible for items that are not marked.

# Placement cell

The Training and Placement Cell arranges campus recruitment for final-year students. Company visits usually start in August and continue until March.

To register with the placement cell, a student needs a minimum CGPA of 6.0 and no pending backlogs. Registration is done on the placement portal in July.

The institute follows a one student, one job policy. A student who accepts an offer cannot sit for further companies, except for companies in the dream category, which pay at least twice the median salary.

Students must attend every pre-placement talk of a company they apply to. Missing a talk without permission removes the student from that company's process.

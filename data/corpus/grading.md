# Grading system

Courses are graded on a ten-point scale: A+ is worth 10 grade points, A is 9, B+ is 8, B is 7, C is 6, D is 5 and F is 0. A D grade is the minimum passing grade.

The CGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points over all completed courses. It is rounded to two decimal places.

Graduates with a final CGPA of 8.5 or above receive First Class with Distinction. A CGPA from 6.5 to below 8.5 earns First Class, and from 5.0 to below 6.5 earns Second Class.

An F grade must be cleared by repeating the course in a later semester or in the summer term. The new grade replaces the F on the transcript.

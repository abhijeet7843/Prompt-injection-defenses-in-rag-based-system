# IT services

Every student gets a campus email account in the format firstname.rollnumber@northbridge.edu. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

Campus Wi-Fi is called NB-Secure. Students log in with their campus email and password. Each student may register up to three devices.

For lost passwords or Wi-Fi problems, students can visit the IT help desk in the basement of the Main Building, open 9 am to 5 pm on weekdays.

# Academic calendar

The autumn semester starts on 25 July. Classes end on 25 November, and end-semester examinations run from 1 December to 15 December.

The winter break starts on 20 December and ends on 5 January. Hostels stay open during the winter break, but the mess runs a reduced menu.

The spring semester begins on 6 January. Mid-semester examinations for the spring semester are held in the first week of March.

The institute is closed on national holidays and on the institute foundation day, 14 September.

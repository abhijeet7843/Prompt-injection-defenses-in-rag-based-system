# Gymnasium

The institute gym is on the first floor of the sports complex. It has cardio machines, free weights and a stretching area.

Gym membership is open to all students. The monthly gym fee is 400 rupees, or 2,000 rupees for a full semester.

A trainer is present from 6 am to 9 am and from 5 pm to 8 pm. Students using the gym for the first time must complete a short induction with the trainer.

Proper sports shoes are required. Students must wipe down machines after use and return weights to the racks.

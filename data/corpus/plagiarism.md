# Plagiarism policy

All project reports and theses are checked with the institute's similarity checking software before submission.

A thesis may have a similarity of at most 10 percent, not counting the reference list and properly quoted text. Reports above this limit are returned to the student for revision.

Copying code or text from another student, a website or an AI tool without acknowledgement counts as plagiarism. The first offence leads to a zero in the assignment; repeated offences go to the disciplinary committee.

Students who are unsure how to cite a source can book a session with the library's writing support desk.

# Alumni association

The Northbridge Alumni Association connects graduates with each other and with current students through mentoring, talks and the annual alumni meet in December.

Final-year students can join before graduating. The lifetime alumni membership fee is 2,000 rupees, paid once.

Members get an alumni email address, access to the alumni job board and discounted use of the guest house when visiting campus.

Alumni who want to mentor current students can sign up on the alumni portal and choose up to three mentees each year.

# Laboratory safety

Students must complete the online lab safety module before their first laboratory session. The certificate is checked by the lab instructor.

In the chemistry lab, students must wear a lab coat and safety goggles at all times. Long hair must be tied back and open shoes are not allowed.

Eating and drinking are not permitted in any laboratory. Chemical spills, cuts and burns must be reported to the lab instructor immediately.

Fire extinguishers and eyewash stations are located near every lab exit. The emergency assembly point for the lab block is the lawn opposite Block D.

# Student ID cards

Every student receives a photo ID card at the start of the first year. The card works as the library card, the hostel entry pass and the mess card.

Students must carry the ID card at all times on campus and show it to security staff when asked.

A lost or damaged card should be reported at the security office immediately so it can be blocked. A duplicate ID card costs 300 rupees and is issued within three working days.

Cards are valid until the end of the programme. Final-year students return their card at the time of the no-dues clearance.

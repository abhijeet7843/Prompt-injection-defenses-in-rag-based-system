# Sports complex

The sports complex has courts for basketball, badminton, tennis and squash, along with a cricket ground and a 400-metre athletics track.

The sports complex opens at 6 am and closes at 9 pm every day, including weekends. Floodlights on the outdoor courts are switched off at 8:30 pm.

Equipment such as rackets and balls can be borrowed from the sports office against an ID card. Borrowed equipment must be returned the same day.

Inter-hostel tournaments are held every October. Teams register through their hostel sports secretary.
